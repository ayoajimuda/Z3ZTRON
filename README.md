## 🤖 A note on AI & the current tech climate

Right now, AI is taking more than it’s giving.

As off now, the tech space is currently oversaturated and under threat from the adoption of Artificial Intelligence by several large tech organization resulting in mass layoffs & more massive unemployment rates, declining software quality, rushed products & mindless innovations that solve nothing, all while companies make claims that *"AI makes people more efficient"* or *"AI is a great tool, it's boosting the economy"* or *"Those who are anti AI are simple not ready for change, they are like the Luddites and we need to move past that/them"*.

Human creativity is being diluted, environmental costs are increasing, and actual infrastructure is being stressed due to excessive energy use, water consumption, and data centre expansion. Simultaneously, the computer industry is being moulded around AI at any costs, causing havoc where it did not need to be. Memory manufacturers are prioritising AI data centres over traditional customers, resulting in shortages and increased costs for developers, gamers, and smaller firms. That is not innovation serving people; it is an environment built upon hype. Not to be misunderstood, but I do believe AI can be a tool used for good reasons in certain areas of our lives, but right now, I'm hoping, praying that the AI bubble to burst, even though I know that it might led to the possibility of a recession, I pray it does so that we might go back to some form of normalcy. Maybe it does. Maybe it does not. However, I believe fundamentals will survive trends.
